CASE FILE — TAKE ACTION

The case is made. Here's how to be heard.

Administration responds to documented, repeated, specific communication — not one-off angry comments. Here's where to direct it.

01 — SEND THE LETTER

A template built on the record, ready to personalize.

Copy this, add a sentence or two in your own words, and send it directly. Personalized letters carry more weight than identical copy-paste text — offices tend to filter mass form campaigns.

Subject: Request for Accountability — Ohio State Football
Dear [Athletic Director Name / "Ohio State Department of Athletics"],
I am writing regarding a documented, seven-year pattern in Ohio State football that I believe warrants action. Since 2019, Ohio State has lost 15 games under Head Coach Ryan Day — 7 of those losses involved squandering a significant lead, most recently a blown 14-0 lead at home against Oregon on November 7, 2026 and a 23-3 lead against Texas on September 12, 2026.
In 2024, the only season without a signature late-game collapse, offensive play-calling was handled independently by an experienced coordinator, Chip Kelly — and that season ended in a national championship. Every other season, this pattern has recurred.
In January 2026, Ohio State hired Arthur Smith as offensive coordinator — a 21-30 record as an NFL head coach, no playoff appearances, and two below-average years as an NFL coordinator, with zero prior college experience. His first marquee game resulted in the collapse described above. On defense, coordinator Matt Patricia’s unit has been part of five losses in two seasons, four of them at neutral or away sites. The offensive line has also struggled in the program’s biggest games, allowing ten sacks in the final two games of 2025.
I am asking the athletic department to part ways with Head Coach Ryan Day. At the very least, I am asking that the coaches responsible for these results, including offensive coordinator Arthur Smith, defensive coordinator Matt Patricia and offensive line coach Tyler Bowen, be replaced.
Respectfully,
[Your Name]
